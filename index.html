About Me

My name is Tom Rongen, and I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at Maastricht University. I am supervised by Frank Cörvers, Inge de Wolf, and Tom Stolp. My research is funded by Education Lab Netherlands and the Dutch Inspectorate of Eduction. My main focus is on teacher shortages in primary education. I exploit both existing administrative datasets and primary data to study how teacher shortages affect pupils and how shortages might be mitigated. If you are interested in my research, feel free to contact me at "tom.rongen@maastrichtuniversity.nl.
