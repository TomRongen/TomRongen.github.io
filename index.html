About Me

I am a PhD researcher specializing in education economics and labour economics. My research focuses on understanding the economic factors that influence educational outcomes and labour market dynamics.

My current work examines the intersection of education policy, teacher recruitment and retention, and broader economic outcomes. I combine quantitative analysis with policy-relevant insights to contribute to evidence-based decision-making in education.
